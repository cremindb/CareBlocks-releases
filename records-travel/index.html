When health records travel

An abbreviation used to be safe because it was local. A health information exchange removes the shared context without removing the shorthand — your note is now opened by someone in a different facility, a different specialty, a different system.

Use CareBlocks on your own workstation

CareBlocks is a free Windows tool that expands abbreviations as you type, warns on Do Not Use terms, and asks which meaning you intended when a term is ambiguous. It runs entirely on your own machine and makes no outbound network calls in its default configuration.

Install from the Microsoft Store

Free. Windows 10 and 11. Installing from the Store means it installs without the SmartScreen prompt.

Measure it in your own documents

Every JCI-accredited hospital already has to audit abbreviation use. Most do it by hand, on a sample of charts, once a year. We are building a free tool that does the same audit on your own exported documents — offline, on your machine, with no document ever leaving it. If you asked for early access, we will be in touch when it is ready.

Findings will be published in aggregate only, with the sample size and method stated alongside every figure. Nothing identifying any person or organisation is ever published.
